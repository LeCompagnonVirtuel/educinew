# GEGIN Governance Framework

## Phase 4.2 - Global Education Intelligence Network

---

## 1. Overview

GEGIN Governance establishes rules, policies, and decision-making structures
for the global education intelligence network across partner institutions.

---

## 2. Governance Model

### 2.1 Decision Tiers

| Tier | Scope | Decision Maker |
|------|-------|----------------|
| Strategic | Network-wide policies | GEGIN Council |
| Tactical | Regional standards | Regional Board |
| Operational | School-level decisions | School Admin |
| Technical | Platform configuration | GEGIN Ops |

### 2.2 Voting Rights

- Each member institution holds one vote
- Weighted voting based on student enrollment tiers
- Quorum: 60% of registered institutions
- Supermajority (75%) for constitutional amendments

---

## 3. Council Structure

### 3.1 GEGIN Council

- Meets quarterly (virtual or in-person)
- Rotating chairperson (annual term)
- Secretariat handles administrative functions
- Minutes published within 14 days

### 3.2 Committees

| Committee | Responsibility |
|-----------|---------------|
| Standards | Curriculum alignment |
| Data | Privacy and interoperability |
| Finance | Budget allocation |
| Ethics | AI and research ethics |
| Security | Cybersecurity posture |

---

## 4. Policy Framework

### 4.1 Policy Lifecycle

```
Draft → Review → Comment → Revision → Vote → Publish → Enforce
```

### 4.2 Policy Categories

- **Mandatory**: Must be implemented by all members
- **Recommended**: Best practices for adoption
- **Optional**: Experimental guidelines

---

## 5. Compliance & Enforcement

### 5.1 Compliance Levels

| Level | Description | Consequence |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Green | Full compliance | None |
| Yellow | Minor deviations | Warning |
| Orange | Significant gaps | Remediation plan |
| Red | Non-compliance | Suspension |

### 5.2 Audit Process

1. Self-assessment submission (annual)
2. Peer review (biennial)
3. External audit (triennial)
4. Corrective action plans as needed

---

## 6. Amendment Process

- Any member institution may propose amendments
- 30-day public comment period required
- Two-thirds council approval for changes
- Effective date: 90 days post-approval

---

## 7. Dispute Resolution

1. **Negotiation**: Direct party resolution (30 days)
2. **Mediation**: Third-party mediator (60 days)
3. **Arbitration**: Binding arbitration panel
4. **Appeal**: GEGIN Council final review

---

## 8. Transparency

- All policies published in GEGIN Portal
- Meeting minutes publicly accessible
- Annual governance report
- Conflict of interest declarations required

---
