# GEGIN Compliance & Regulatory

## Phase 4.2 - Global Education Intelligence Network

---

## 1. Overview

Manages regulatory compliance, data protection, and legal requirements
across jurisdictions in the GEGIN network.

---

## 2. Compliance Framework

| Domain | Scope | Priority |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Data Protection | GDPR, CCPA, etc. | Critical |
| Education | National standards | High |
| Financial | Tax, reporting | High |
| Accessibility | WCAG, ADA | High |
| Security | ISO 27001, SOC2 | Critical |
| Employment | Labor laws | Medium |

---

## 3. Data Protection

### 3.1 Privacy Regulations

| Regulation | Jurisdiction | Key Requirements |
|------------|--------------|------------------|
| GDPR | EU/EEA | Consent, rights, DPO |
| CCPA | California | Opt-out, disclosure |
| PIPEDA | Canada | Consent, limits |
| POPIA | South Africa | Conditions, rights |
| LGPD | Brazil | Consent, DPO |

### 3.2 Data Subject Rights

- **Access**: Right to obtain data
- **Rectification**: Right to correct
- **Erasure**: Right to be forgotten
- **Portability**: Right to transfer
- **Restriction**: Right to limit processing
- **Objection**: Right to oppose

---

## 4. Consent Management

### 4.1 Consent Principles

1. **Freely given**: No coercion
2. **Specific**: Purpose-bound
3. **Informed**: Clear explanation
4. **Unambiguous**: Affirmative action
5. **Withdrawable**: Easy withdrawal
